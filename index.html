Our Mission

Every child deserves a learning path built for how they experience the world.

NDTP exists to remove the financial barriers that keep autistic and neurodivergent children from the educational and therapeutic support that can help them thrive.

Donations are directed toward tuition assistance, specialized tutoring, therapy sessions, classroom accommodations, and practical guidance for families navigating complex care systems.

What Donations Make Possible

Focused support where families often need it most.

NDTP prioritizes flexible funding, because each child's needs can look different across school, home, and therapy.

Schooling

Tuition assistance for programs equipped to support autistic and neurodivergent learners.

Tutoring

One-to-one learning support that respects attention, sensory needs, and communication style.

Therapy

Access to therapy sessions and developmental support that build confidence and daily-life skills.

Family Guidance

Help understanding options, paperwork, and next steps when support systems feel hard to navigate.

Impact Model

A donation becomes a practical bridge.

NDTP turns community generosity into direct assistance for education and care. The aim is simple: reduce pressure on families and widen access to the support children need.

01 Families request support

NDTP identifies schooling or therapy costs creating the biggest barrier.

02 Funds are allocated

Donations are directed toward tuition, tutoring, therapy, or accommodations.

03 Children receive continuity

Stable support gives children more room to learn, communicate, and grow.
